Everything You Need

Amenities & Services

Breakfast, Wi-Fi, housekeeping, the gym and the rooftop pool are included in every room rate — no extra charge, no surprises at checkout.

Stay Active

Fitness Gym

A fully equipped gym with free weights, kettlebells, cardio machines and a dedicated stretching area — open daily and free for all room guests, no separate booking required.

Always On

24/7 Reception & Security

Our front desk is staffed around the clock, every day of the year — for late arrivals, early departures, or anything you need in between. The property is monitored 24/7 for your peace of mind.

Arrive Stress-Free

Airport Shuttle

Arrange a transfer to or from Mwanza Airport in advance and we'll have a car waiting — one less thing to plan on a travel day.

Unwind Outdoors

Garden Courtyards

A quiet, planted courtyard right at the entrance — a small green pause between the street and the lobby, and a favourite backdrop for guest photos.

Cool Off

Rooftop Swimming Pool

Take a break from the Mwanza heat at our rooftop pool — open daily for guests, with a dedicated Pool Side deck for anyone who'd rather relax than swim.

  • Open daily for all room guests
  • Rooftop deck & poolside seating
  • Towels provided at the front desk
