Our Story

Built on Tanzanian Hospitality

Taiwan Hotel opened its doors in Mwanza in 2018 with a simple aim: give travellers — whether visiting for business, a wedding, or a lakeside break — a place that feels like home rather than a transaction. Six years on, that's still the promise every member of our team makes to every guest who walks through the door.

We're proud to be a Tanzanian-run hotel that grew from the ground up in this city — a destination in its own right, not just a place to sleep between meetings.

Doing Our Part

Thoughtful Hospitality

Good hospitality looks after more than just guests. We run on solar-assisted water heating, keep real plants throughout the property instead of disposable decor, and work with local suppliers for our kitchen and housekeeping — so your stay supports Mwanza's own economy, not just ours.

It's a small, honest step rather than a marketing claim, and one we keep building on every year.
