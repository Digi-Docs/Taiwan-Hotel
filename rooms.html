Room 01 · For One

Single Room

Compact, calm and considered — our Single is built around one comfortable double bed dressed in crisp white linen. A wall-mounted flat-screen faces the bed, and a timber bedside console holds the tea and coffee tray, a direct-dial room phone and complimentary bottled water.

The en-suite is finished floor-to-ceiling in tile, with an instant hot-water shower and fresh towels every day. Layered blackout and sheer curtains let you decide how early the morning starts.

  • En-suite · instant hot water
  • Flat-screen TV
  • Tea & coffee tray
  • Room telephone
  • Free Wi-Fi

Room 02 · For Two

Twin Room

A generously sized bed dressed in crisp linen beneath a draped mosquito-net canopy, set against a full mirrored wall that opens the room up and doubles the light. A small desk and chair give you somewhere to work in the evening, and a hardwood wardrobe keeps luggage out from underfoot.

The en-suite is where this room quietly earns its keep — a rainfall shower alongside a freestanding soaking tub, finished in the same warm stone tile as the rest of the house.

Room 03 · For Three

Triple Room

Three beds, generously spaced, in a room wide enough that nobody is climbing over anyone's luggage. It's our most practical choice for families travelling together, colleagues sharing costs, or a small group in town for a wedding or conference.

The bathroom is the quiet luxury here — a bath tub alongside an overhead rain shower, monogrammed Taiwan Hotel towels on a heated-style rail, and hot water on demand at any hour.

  • Three beds
  • Bath tub & rain shower
  • Air-conditioned
  • Flat-screen TV
  • Extra towels & linen
  • Free Wi-Fi
